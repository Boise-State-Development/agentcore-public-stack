This is a mix shift, not a saving: one Agent served 9,234 single-call turns to 592 users in the last week at about $0.013 a turn. Without each week's highest-volume Agent the turn count is flat (8,557 → 8,561) and cost per turn fell 12% ($0.090 → $0.079). The same Agent's volume pulls turn p90 down (48.8 s → 24.9 s); without it p90 still fell 25% (56.8 s → 42.5 s) and p50 from 13.3 s to 9.9 s.
- Model mix: Sonnet 5.5 went from nothing to 26% of the last week's spend; Sonnet 5 fell from 72% to 43%; Opus 5.5 rose from 23% to 27%. Part of the 12% is this move.
- Share of tool turns with an error fell 10% (13.7% → 12.3%), in step with the `analyze_spreadsheet`, `policy-search` and `search_files` fixes.
- Copied share recovered from 0.5% to 1.0%, so last week's drop was not a broken signal.
- Unexplained cache waste fell from $14 to $12 in the week, and from $99 to $84 over 28 days.
- Median first token is flat at 2.0 s. By release, 1.37.0 reads 1.5 s and 1.38.0 reads 2.8 s, but 1.38.0 has 169 turns from part of one day: no evidence yet.
