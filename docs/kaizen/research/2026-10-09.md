### What's moving this week

The week's shape: **Anthropic shipped the model we'd most want as a default, and documented the rule that makes it hardest to adopt.** Haiku 5.5 (10-07) is on Bedrock with a confirmed `global.*` id. No `us.*` id has been confirmed, and that matters because the dev SCP blocks `global.*`. The same day, Sonnet 5.5's cache-read price dropped from $0.20 to $0.10/MTok (0.05× input). "Simple" compaction, meaning one summary message and no earlier assistant turns, passes, and so does server-side compaction. Everything in our cost strategy that rewrites history (compaction, the tool-result offloader) is now a correctness question on four curated or curatable models, not just a cost one.

The second theme is **upstream moving faster than our pins, and taking voice with it twice.** Strands shipped 1.58.0, 1.58.1 and 1.59.0 in four days. 1.58.0 removed `ModalityUsage` and changed Bidi usage to snake_case per-event deltas. Our voice agent imports `ModalityUsage` inside an `except ImportError` that flips `BIDI_AVAILABLE=False`, so the next bump turns voice off silently. This is the second Strands release in a row with a voice-only break. The good news is on the caching watch: #4618 hasn't moved, `bedrock.py` still passes usage through untouched at 1.59.0, and Strands now splits Nova speech and text itself, so our override can probably be deleted rather than ported.

Third, and quieter: **MCP standardised discovery.** SEP-2127 (Server Cards, `.well-known/ai-catalog.json`) merged on 10-06. FastMCP 4.1.0 made HTTP sessions expire after 30 idle minutes, which is a breaking default for any host that holds a session id across a lull.
