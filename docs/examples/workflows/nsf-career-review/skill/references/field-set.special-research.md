CAREER proposal audit: Special Research. Each criterion is pass, fail, cant_tell or not_applicable.

| name | title | type | find | enum |
|------|-------|------|------|------|
| human_subjects | Human subjects | enum | If the project involves human subjects, IRB approval or exemption is stated. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| vertebrate_animals | Vertebrate animals | enum | If the project uses vertebrate animals, IACUC approval is stated. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| field_permits | Field permits | enum | Permits for work on public or tribal land (for example well installation) are named, with their status. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| drone_compliance | Drone flights compliant | enum | Any drone or UAS flights name the FAA authority they fly under (for example Part 107). | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| international_activities | International activities | enum | Any international activities are identified with the countries involved. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| environmental_impact | Environmental impact | enum | The proposal says whether the fieldwork may affect the environment and how that is handled. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
