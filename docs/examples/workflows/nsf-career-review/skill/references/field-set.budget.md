CAREER proposal audit: Budget. Each criterion is pass, fail, cant_tell or not_applicable.

| name | title | type | find | enum |
|------|-------|------|------|------|
| five_year_budget | Five annual budgets | enum | The budget has a column or table for each of the five years and a cumulative total. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| minimum_total | Meets the CAREER minimum | enum | The cumulative total request is at least $400,000. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| totals_add_up | Totals add up | enum | Each year's line items add up to that year's total, and the years add up to the cumulative total. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| senior_salary_two_months | Senior salary at most two months, or justified | enum | Senior personnel salary is at most two months a year in total, or the budget justification explains why more is needed. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| fringe_rates_stated | Fringe rates stated | enum | The budget justification states the fringe benefit rates used. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| fa_rate_stated | F&A rate stated | enum | The budget justification states the indirect cost (F&A) rate and its basis. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| no_fa_on_participant_support | No F&A on participant support | enum | Indirect costs (F&A) are not charged on participant support costs. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| participant_support_justified | Participant support justified | enum | Participant support costs are itemized and explained in the justification. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| equipment_threshold | Equipment over $5,000 a unit | enum | Every item listed as equipment costs at least $5,000 a unit. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| equipment_justified | Equipment justified | enum | Each equipment item is explained in the justification. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| travel_justified | Travel justified | enum | Each trip in the budget is explained with its purpose and destination. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| justification_covers_categories | Justification covers every category | enum | The budget justification covers every budget category that has a nonzero amount. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| justification_length | Justification within five pages | enum | The budget justification is at most five pages. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
