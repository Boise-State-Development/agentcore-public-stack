CAREER proposal audit: Reporting. Each criterion is pass, fail, cant_tell or not_applicable.

| name | title | type | find | enum |
|------|-------|------|------|------|
| measurable_outcomes | Measurable outcomes | enum | The proposal names measurable outcomes that annual reports can track. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| education_metrics | Education metrics | enum | The education plan names how many students or participants it will reach. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| assessment_instrument | Assessment instrument | enum | The evaluation plan names its instrument or method (for example a survey before and after). | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| external_evaluator | Evaluator named | enum | The person or office that evaluates the education activities is named. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| annual_report_content | Annual reporting planned | enum | The proposal says what it will report each year. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| dissemination_plan | Dissemination plan | enum | The proposal names where results will be published or presented. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| public_outcomes | Public outcomes | enum | The proposal plans a public-facing outcome or project page. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| participant_tracking | Participant tracking | enum | The proposal says how it will track participants in its education activities. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
