CAREER proposal audit: Post-Award. Each criterion is pass, fail, cant_tell or not_applicable.

| name | title | type | find | enum |
|------|-------|------|------|------|
| data_sharing_timeline | Data sharing timeline | enum | The proposal says when the data will be made available (for example within a year of collection). | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| equipment_ownership | Equipment after the award | enum | The proposal says what happens to the equipment it buys after the award ends. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| site_restoration | Sites restored | enum | The proposal says how field sites (wells, instruments) will be removed or restored at the end. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| no_cost_extension_not_assumed | No extension assumed | enum | The timeline does not depend on a no-cost extension. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| subawards_identified | Subawards identified | enum | Any subaward is identified with its organization and budget. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| partner_commitments | Partner commitments | enum | Each external partner's commitment is stated and backed by a letter. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| rebudgeting_flagged | Major rebudgeting not planned | enum | The plan does not depend on moving funds between budget categories later. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| key_personnel_changes | Continuity of key personnel | enum | The proposal says how the work continues if a key student leaves. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
