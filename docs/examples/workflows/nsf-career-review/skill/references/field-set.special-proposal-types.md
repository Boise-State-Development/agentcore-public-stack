CAREER proposal audit: Special Proposal Types. Each criterion is pass, fail, cant_tell or not_applicable.

| name | title | type | find | enum |
|------|-------|------|------|------|
| proposal_type_career | Submitted as CAREER | enum | The proposal identifies itself as a Faculty Early Career Development (CAREER) proposal. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| duration_five_years | Five-year duration | enum | The requested duration is five years. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| not_collaborative | Not a collaborative proposal | enum | The proposal is not split across institutions as a collaborative proposal. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| not_rapid_eager | Not RAPID or EAGER | enum | The proposal does not also present itself as a RAPID or EAGER. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| career_development_plan | Career development plan | enum | The proposal describes the PI's career goals and how the project builds a foundation for them. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
| research_education_integration | Research and education integration | enum | The proposal explains how its research and education activities inform each other. | pass, fail, cant_tell, not_applicable |
