AgentCore Public Stack is the AI platform for **public institutions on AWS**:
universities, public research and state and local government. It packages **AWS Bedrock AgentCore** and **Strands
Agents** into a complete product: a chat experience, custom agents and skills,
shared team workspaces, document intelligence, voice, a terminal client, and an
admin console for identity, access, quotas and cost. It deploys into an AWS
account you control, governs every model, tool and connector by role, and
[publishes its eval results](/agentcore-public-stack/benchmarks/) on every
release. It is source-available, and free for universities, public research and
government.

It is the working codebase behind the AI platform that serves students, faculty
and staff at Boise State University. Fork it, deploy it, brand it, and hand your
whole organization an AI platform you own, without writing agent code: models,
tools, roles and policies are configured in the admin console.

## Who it's for

The platform is general-purpose, but it was shaped by the needs of a public
university and the agencies it works alongside.

## What you get

- **A finished conversation surface.** Token-by-token streaming with tool calls,
  interactive tool UIs and errors on one stream; artifacts in a docked side panel;
  voice mode and dictation; conversation search, pinning and mid-turn steering;
  automatic compaction of long conversations.
- **Agents and skills.** The Agent Designer composes instructions, a model,
  tools, skills and a knowledge base into an agent, and the marketplace publishes
  it with reach controls and version snapshots. Skills are portable instruction
  bundles triggered with `/`. See [Agents](/agentcore-public-stack/features/agents/).
- **Shared Projects.** A workspace with owner, editor and viewer roles, one
  assistant, shared files and memory, and schedules. See
  [Projects](/agentcore-public-stack/features/projects/).
- **Documents and knowledge.** Per-agent and per-project knowledge bases on
  Amazon Bedrock with scheduled sync, documents up to 50 MB read by reference,
  Drive folder attachments and Word tracked changes. See
  [Knowledge Bases](/agentcore-public-stack/features/rag/).
- **Every integration in one registry.** Direct tools, AWS SDK calls, MCP servers
  behind AgentCore Gateway, external MCP servers and remote A2A agents, with
  [MCP Apps](/agentcore-public-stack/features/mcp-apps/) and OAuth consent
  mid-conversation.
- **Three ways in.** The web app, the
  [terminal client](/agentcore-public-stack/features/terminal-client/), and
  [API keys](/agentcore-public-stack/features/api-keys/), all under the same
  roles and quotas.
- **Governance.** Roles from your identity provider, delegated admin scopes,
  direct user grants, an audit log, quota tiers and cost analytics down to the
  model call. See the [Admin Overview](/agentcore-public-stack/admin/overview/).

## Why an institutional AI platform?

Commercial AI subscriptions are convenient, but at the scale of a campus or a
workforce they lock you into someone else's roadmap, pricing and data practices.
Running your own platform on infrastructure you control changes that calculus.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="No per-seat licensing">
		Per-seat pricing grows with every person you add. At 30,000 users,
		commercial subscriptions would cost upwards of $7.2M a year. Here you pay for
		the tokens people use, through the AWS account and agreement you already have.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Data under your control">
		Every conversation, document and knowledge base lives inside your AWS account.
		Models are used for inference only and never train on your data.
	</Card>
	<Card title="No vendor lock-in">
		Choose and swap the best model for each task, from Bedrock, OpenAI and Gemini,

		up-front capital cost.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Equitable access">
		Everyone gets the same capable models and tools. Premium capability is a role
		grant, not a price tier.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Systems integration">
		Connect the systems your people rely on through MCP servers registered in the
		admin console, so the assistant can act on real data within your controls.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Accountability">
		Role-based access from your identity provider, delegated admin scopes, an
		audit log of every admin change, and cost attribution down to the individual
		model call.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Budgets everyone can see

Consumption billing only works for an institution if spend is predictable per
person and visible before it becomes a problem. Quotas are enforced in dollars on
every conversational surface:

- A **tier** sets a monthly limit and an optional daily one, and chooses whether
  reaching it blocks the next turn or only warns.
- Tiers attach to a role, an identity-provider claim, an email domain or an
  individual, so a new user lands in the right tier on first sign-in.
- Users are warned **in the conversation** as they approach the limit, and when
  one conversation alone has spent a notable share of the month. A blocked turn is
  a message that says when the quota resets, never an error.
- Admins grant **timed overrides**, see exactly which tier applied to a user in
  the **quota inspector**, and export the log of every warning and block.

Quota requests and extensions, with chargeback to a cost center, grant or
department fund, are in development; see
[Quotas](/agentcore-public-stack/admin/quotas/) and the
[roadmap](/agentcore-public-stack/roadmap/).
