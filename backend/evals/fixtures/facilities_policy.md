# Harrowfield Community College — Facilities Use Policy

*Synthetic document for the evaluation harness. The college, its rooms, forms and
figures are invented.*

## 1. Scope

This policy covers bookings of teaching rooms, meeting rooms and outdoor spaces on
the Harrowfield main campus by staff, students and approved community groups.

## 2. Room bookings

Room requests must be submitted at least **72 hours** before the start of the
event through the facilities portal. Requests for portable audio-visual equipment
follow a separate rule: they need **48 hours** of notice, because the equipment is
shared with the evening programme.

Each room has a posted maximum occupancy. The seminar room **B214** holds at most
**38** people. Do not confuse it with the lecture hall **B241**, which holds **52**.

## 3. Access outside opening hours

The campus buildings are open from 7:00 to 21:30 on weekdays. Access outside these
hours requires form **FAC-17** (After-Hours Access Request), signed by a department
head. Form **FAC-71** is the key-loan form and does not grant after-hours access.

## 4. Cancellations and charges

A booking cancelled less than 24 hours before its start incurs a late cancellation
fee of **$35**. Damage to rooms or furniture is charged separately at a minimum of
**$53** per incident, plus the cost of repair.

## 5. Contacts

Questions about this policy go to the Facilities Coordination Desk at extension
**4417**. Security, for emergencies only, is at extension **4000**.
